About Bohlale Hoodies, Your Local Hoodie Brand

Bohlale Hoodies started in 2019 printing custom hoodies for friends and student groups. We now design our own range of hoodies, zip-ups and jerseys in soft, heavyweight fabric, available from three shops: Menlyn in Pretoria, Rosebank in Johannesburg and Mall of the North in Polokwane.

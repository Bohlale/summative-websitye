Our Story

Bohlale means wisdom, and we believe good clothing should be made to last. We began in 2019 printing hoodies for friends and student groups, and the demand for a hoodie that stays soft and holds its shape kept growing.

Today we design every piece ourselves and sell it in sizes S to 3XL from three shops in Pretoria, Johannesburg and Polokwane, and online.
